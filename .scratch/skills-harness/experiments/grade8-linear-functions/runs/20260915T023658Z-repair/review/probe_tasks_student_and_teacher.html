Teacher Guide: Task PROBE-1

Mathematical Solutions

  1. Dimensions of △ABD\triangle ABD△ABD:
    • Δx1=4−1=3\Delta x_1 = 4 - 1 = 3Δx1​=4−1=3 units.
    • Δy1=9−3=6\Delta y_1 = 9 - 3 = 6Δy1​=9−3=6 units.
    • Ratio: Δy1Δx1=63=2\frac{\Delta y_1}{\Delta x_1} = \frac{6}{3} = 2Δx1​Δy1​​=36​=2.
  2. Dimensions of △BCE\triangle BCE△BCE:
    • Δx2=8−4=4\Delta x_2 = 8 - 4 = 4Δx2​=8−4=4 units.
    • Δy2=17−9=8\Delta y_2 = 17 - 9 = 8Δy2​=17−9=8 units.
    • Ratio: Δy2Δx2=84=2\frac{\Delta y_2}{\Delta x_2} = \frac{8}{4} = 2Δx2​Δy2​​=48​=2.
  3. Geometric Explanation:
    • Angle Equality: Both triangles have a right angle (∠ADB=∠BEC=90∘\angle ADB = \angle BEC = 90^\circ∠ADB=∠BEC=90∘) because the legs are parallel to the perpendicular coordinate axes. The line acts as a transversal intersecting horizontal lines y=3y = 3y=3 and y=9y = 9y=9. Corresponding angles ∠DAB\angle DAB∠DAB and ∠EBC\angle EBC∠EBC are congruent.
    • AA Similarity: By Angle-Angle (AA) similarity criterion (8.G.A.5), △ABD∼△BCE\triangle ABD \sim \triangle BCE△ABD∼△BCE.
    • Conclusion: Corresponding side lengths of similar triangles are proportional: DBAD=ECBE  ⟹  63=84=2\frac{DB}{AD} = \frac{EC}{BE} \implies \frac{6}{3} = \frac{8}{4} = 2ADDB​=BEEC​⟹36​=48​=2. Therefore, the ratio of vertical change to horizontal change is invariant between any two points on the line.
  4. Derivation of Equation:
    • At x=0x = 0x=0, y=3−2(1)=1y = 3 - 2(1) = 1y=3−2(1)=1. The vertical intercept is (0,1)(0, 1)(0,1), so b=1b = 1b=1.
    • Equation: y=2x+1y = 2x + 1y=2x+1.
    • Verification with C(8,17)C(8, 17)C(8,17): Substitute x=8x = 8x=8: y=2(8)+1=16+1=17y = 2(8) + 1 = 16 + 1 = 17y=2(8)+1=16+1=17. The equation holds true.
